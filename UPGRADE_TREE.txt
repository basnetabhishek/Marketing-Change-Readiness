MARKETING CHANGE READINESS - UPGRADE TREE

DESCRIPTION
Marketing Change Readiness helps teams find marketing content that may become
outdated when a product offer changes. It combines deterministic checks,
scope-aware evidence scanning, automated monitoring, and grounded AI review so
teams can identify affected claims and reduce unnecessary manual review.
